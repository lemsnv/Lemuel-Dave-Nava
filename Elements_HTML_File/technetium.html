Technetium

Atomic Weight: 97[note]
Density: 11.5 g/cm3
Melting Point: 2157 °C
Boiling Point: 4265 °C

Technetium is a radioactive element, the only one in this area of the periodic table. Short-lived isotopes are used in gamma ray imaging of the skeleton since it attaches itself to areas of active bone growth.
