Phosporus

Atomic Weight: 30.973761998
Density: 1.823 g/cm3
Melting Point: 44.2 °C[note]
Boiling Point: 280.5 °C[note]

The Phosphorus occurs in white (extremely dangerous), red (safer and common in matches) and black (rare, most stable) forms. This exotic violet form is a mixture of red and black, not a true allotrope itself.
