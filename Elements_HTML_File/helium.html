Helium

Atomic Weight: 4.002602
Density: 0.1785 g/l[note]
Melting Point: N/A[note]
Boiling Point: -268.93 °C

Ordinarily a colorless, inert gas, helium glows pale peach when an electric current runs through it. A glass blower bent a tube to the shape of the letters He and filled it with pure helium. I lit it with high voltage.
